# Demo video script — 2 minutes or less

Language of the video: **Arabic** (voice-over below). Total: **1:55**, leaving 5 seconds of margin under the 2-minute limit. Every number said in the video is measured (`docs/EVALUATION.md`, `docs/HARD_CASES.md`); do not add any other number. **Record after the final deployment** and use the live link.

What is true and may be said: an AI model reads the isnād **in the browser**; matching and ranking are fixed calculations; every result is a real corpus hadith, labelled «يحتاج تحققًا — من مجموعة خارجية»; the model and the corpus have unclear licences (said openly, once). **Do not say** a hadith is sound or weak, that a match is "the same person", or that imported hadiths are verified.

## Recording instructions (for the owner)

1. **Prepare (5 min).** Open the live site in a new private window of Edge or Chrome, full screen (F11), 1440 × 900 or larger, zoom 100 %. Close other tabs and notifications (Windows: Focus assist on). **Open the site once and wait for the model to load before recording**, so shot 2 is not a long wait.
2. **Record.** Windows: **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar), files go to `Videos\Captures`; or OBS Studio. Record each shot separately; retakes are cheap.
3. **Voice.** Record the voice-over separately in a quiet room (a phone voice recorder is fine), one file per shot. If a shot runs long, cut words, not speed.
4. **Edit.** Windows **Clipchamp**: put the clips in order, lay the voice files under them, trim each shot to its time, add the shot 6 slide as a title card. Arabic subtitles if time allows.
5. **Check before upload:** total ≤ 2:00 · no personal data on screen (no browser profile name, no e-mail) · no number except those in shot 6 and the corpus count · the live link visible at the end.
6. **Export** 1080p MP4 and upload where the submission form asks.
